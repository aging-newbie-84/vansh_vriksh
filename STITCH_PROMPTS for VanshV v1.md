# Vansh Vriksha — Google Stitch Screen Prompts

Use each prompt below as-is in Google Stitch to generate UI mockups for each screen.

## Screen 1 — Home / Welcome Screen

```
A warm, ornate Indian heritage app welcome screen. Deep maroon (#8B1A1A) header 
with gold paisley border pattern. Aged parchment (#FDF3E3) background. 
Center: large stylised banyan tree logo in gold. App name "वंश वृक्ष / Vansh Vriksha" 
in antique English serif font. Subtitle "Map your family. Honour your roots." 
Below: two large rounded buttons — "Start Speaking" with microphone icon 
and "Type Your Story" with pen icon. Both buttons in deep maroon with gold text. 
Footer in dark ink with small lotus divider motif. Feels like a temple notice board 
meets a royal decree. Dignified, warm, not digital-looking.
```
